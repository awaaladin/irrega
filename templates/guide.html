{# ---- the four decisions ---- #}

The four decisions

Every decision has its own colour, icon and words, so you never rely on colour alone.

  • {% include "components/action_badge.html" with action="IRRIGATE_NOW" big=True %}

    The soil has already used up the water the crop can spare, and rain will not cover it. Water in the next cool window.

  • {% include "components/action_badge.html" with action="IRRIGATE_LATER_TODAY" big=True %}

    Fine right now but it will reach the limit by tonight. We give you the best window, usually late afternoon.

  • {% include "components/action_badge.html" with action="SKIP_TODAY" big=True %}

    The soil holds enough, or rain is likely to do the job. Skipping saves water and time, and protects roots from wet soil.

  • {% include "components/action_badge.html" with action="WATCH" big=True %}

    Something does not add up, for example the crop is losing greenness in already wet soil. Look for disease, pests, drainage or hunger in the soil.

What we are honest about

  • {% icon "flag" "h-5 w-5" %}Anything simulated is labelled Demo data or Simulated. We never show made-up readings as real.
  • {% icon "flag" "h-5 w-5" %}The advice is a transparent water-balance calculation, not a black box. It is a decision aid, not a guarantee. Your eyes on the crop still matter.
  • {% icon "flag" "h-5 w-5" %}When data is missing or old we say so and lower our confidence instead of pretending.
  • {% icon "flag" "h-5 w-5" %}Water saved is an estimate against a stated baseline: watering every day at the crop's daily need.
