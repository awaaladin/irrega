{# ---------- how it works ---------- #}

How it works, in four steps

  1. 1 · Tell it about your land

    Pin your farm on the map, then add each field: the crop, size, soil type, planting date and how you water. The app works out the growth stage by itself.

  2. 2 · It gathers the evidence

    Weather forecast for your exact spot, your soil moisture reading, crop greenness from satellite, and how last season's harvest went.

  3. 3 · You get one decision per field

    How much water, when to give it, and how sure we are. Open "Why this?" to see every reason and what happens if you wait a day.

  4. 4 · You decide

    Accept the advice, do it your own way, or snooze it. Everything is saved, and the app only ever suggests changes. It never changes things silently.

{# ---------- the four decisions ---------- #}

The four decisions you will see

Each decision has its own colour, its own icon and its own words, so you never have to rely on colour alone.

  • {% include "components/action_badge.html" with action="IRRIGATE_NOW" big=True %}

    The soil has already used up the water the crop can spare, and rain will not cover it. Water in the next cool window, early morning or evening.

  • {% include "components/action_badge.html" with action="IRRIGATE_LATER_TODAY" big=True %}

    The soil is fine this minute but will reach the limit by tonight. We tell you the best window, usually late afternoon, so you avoid the midday heat.

  • {% include "components/action_badge.html" with action="SKIP_TODAY" big=True %}

    The soil holds enough, or rain is likely to do the job. Skipping saves water, time and money, and keeps roots from sitting in wet soil.

  • {% include "components/action_badge.html" with action="WATCH" big=True %}

    Something does not add up. For example the crop is losing greenness but the soil is already wet, so more water will not help. Look for disease, pests, drainage or hunger in the soil.

What we are honest about

  • {% icon "flag" "h-5 w-5" %}Anything simulated is labelled Demo data or Simulated. We never show made-up readings as real.
  • {% icon "flag" "h-5 w-5" %}The advice comes from a transparent water-balance calculation, not a black box. It is a decision aid, not a guarantee, and your own eyes on the crop still matter.
  • {% icon "flag" "h-5 w-5" %}When data is missing or old, we say so and lower our confidence instead of pretending.
  • {% icon "flag" "h-5 w-5" %}Water saved is an estimate against a stated baseline: watering every day at the crop's daily need.
